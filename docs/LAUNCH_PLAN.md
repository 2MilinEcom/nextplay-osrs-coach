# From first MVP to a credible game coach

## Product wedge

Start with OSRS LMS post-fight review: “Understand the decision behind your next improvement.” The valuable loop is capture → inspect evidence → choose one habit → review whether it changes. Avoid pitching mathematical optimality or universal support before measuring it.

## Before a small closed beta

- Complete real-device recording and playback tests and correct timestamp offsets against real clips.
- Connect an authorized AI account and evaluate actual costs and latency per review.
- Have an experienced OSRS PvP player label a small permissioned set of wins and losses. Include ambiguous clips, intentional pressure into protection, tick-delay effects and no-food situations.
- Measure whether review claims are supported, whether the suggested action was available, and whether readers understand uncertainty. Do not substitute model confidence for measured accuracy.
- Complete RuneLite integration build/review before enabling client-connected distribution. Keep video/manual review available independently.
- Add resumable uploads, automatic orphan cleanup, user storage quotas, error monitoring, data retention and account deletion before inviting many users.

## Closed beta

Recruit 5–10 LMS players with consent to review their clips. Track: successful capture rate, reviewed sessions per player, useful versus misleading findings, repeat use after one week, and practice completion. Ask players whether a review taught them something specific. These are proposed validation targets, not existing traction or research findings.

## Paid product gates

Only after review quality and recurring use are demonstrated: introduce public account onboarding, clear recording/privacy controls, payment processing, plan quotas, support and a measured retention experiment. No payment provider is connected in this build.

## Expansion sequence

1. Improve OSRS LMS evidence and repeatable coaching.
2. Broaden OSRS account builds and modes with explicit schemas and uncertainty.
3. Implement one Minecraft mode/version/server adapter; the existing app shell, video pipeline and player profile can be reused.
4. Explore COD Mobile through post-game video review and verify current game/provider rules before deeper integration.
5. Consider any live guidance only after game-specific policy review. Never equate “visible on screen” with automatic permission for a real-time overlay.

## The claim boundary

This app can currently record/import clips, save observations, run understandable rules, organize sessions and support practice. It is not yet an autonomous always-on expert, an exact death-causation engine, or a proven improvement system. The next evidence is a real recorded LMS session, not more dashboard metrics.
